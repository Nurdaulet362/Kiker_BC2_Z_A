# Learning product rebuild

The user rejected the existing interface and asked for a complete rebuild. This replaces the default experience with a learning workspace. Existing report exercises remain available through explicit legacy routes and their saved data is retained.

## Product contract

- Job: learn to spot, repair and explain misleading business reports through short, self-contained work assignments.
- Entry action: begin the recommended case immediately. Returning learners can resume saved drafts.
- Learning loop: read a brief, investigate editable data, repair a metric, answer a transfer question on a different situation, review evidence, practice the weakest skill.
- Six authored bilingual cases cover revenue, conversion and evidence-based budget decisions. Completion and recommendations come from actual submissions. No simulated students, streaks, employment claims or generated AI feedback.
- Hierarchy: learning navigation, one recommended assignment, curriculum, measured progress. An assignment gives the data and task priority over navigation.
- Local reference observation: the old root page presents one long assessment before any learning progression. Its dense worksheet, 30-check review and retry loop obscure what to learn next. The new design separates a short lesson from its transfer question and makes the next learning action visible.

## Visual direction

An approachable learning studio: warm white, deep blue-gray text, coral actions, pale sage and sky skill illustrations. A compact left rail anchors a spacious curriculum. Small spreadsheet/chart drawings express the subject. No photographic filler or oversized marketing sections. DM Sans and Lucide stay locally installed. All new classes are scoped with `learn-` or `lr-`; legacy CSS remains for existing routes.

## Verification contract

Target viewports: 1440x900 and 375x812, plus overflow checks at 320px. Verify the complete lesson-to-recommendation loop, changed dataset/formula behavior, language switching mid-draft, persisted results, keyboard operation and visible focus. Scores describe the exercises only.

States: calculations show pending status; new accounts show zero progress with a start action; invalid formulas/data give actionable errors; partially completed lessons resume without claiming mastery; successful results offer the next exercise. No account permissions are required. Storage failures show a local persistence warning while in-tab work remains usable.
